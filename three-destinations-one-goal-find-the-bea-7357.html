Pueblo Bonito Pacifica Golf & Spa Resort

Cabo Pacifica S/N, Cabo San Lucas, BCS 23450 · Paraíso Escondido
All Inclusive · Adults Only · 4.5-star · 9.2/10 "Wonderful" (1,498 reviews)
Check-in: October 11, 2026  ·  Check-out: October 16, 2026

The resort sits in the mountains on a private beach along the Pacific, with ocean views where whales breach offshore in season. It's built around pools with swim-up bars and cabanas, five restaurants (including an oceanview sushi specialist), and a full-service spa with six treatment rooms. Guests rate cleanliness at 9.6/10, service at 9.4/10, and amenities at 9.2/10.

What's included in the stay

  • Access to four restaurants and three bars/lounges — breakfast, lunch, and dinner service
  • Coffee shop/café, poolside bar, swim-up bar, and snack bar/deli
  • Coffee and tea in common areas
  • Free WiFi in rooms and public areas
  • Self-parking and valet parking on site
  • Daily housekeeping and turndown service

Restaurants on site

  • Siempre — oceanview, breakfast/lunch/dinner, alfresco dining, reservations required
  • Pescados — oceanview sushi specialist, lunch/dinner
  • Aire — beachfront, lunch/dinner, alfresco dining
  • Bocados — poolside coffee shop, breakfast/lunch/dinner
  • Horizons — beachfront, international cuisine, dinner only

On-site amenities

  • Two outdoor pools, one hot tub, sun loungers and umbrellas
  • Fitness facility and beach yoga; two outdoor tennis courts with lessons; beach volleyball; golf course
  • Full-service spa: aromatherapy, Ayurvedic treatments, deep-tissue/hot stone/Swedish massage, facials, manicures/pedicures, reflexology, thalassotherapy, hydrotherapy, mud bath, sauna, steam room
  • 24-hour front desk, ATM, gift shop, grocery/convenience store, hair salon, laundry, luggage storage, in-room safe
  • Concierge, multilingual staff, porter/bellhop, tour and ticket assistance, wedding services

Getting around

  • Airport: San José del Cabo (SJD) — about 48 minutes' drive
  • A car is recommended for getting to and from the property; a roundtrip airport shuttle is available for a surcharge, running 8:00 AM–8:00 PM
  • Nearby: Quivira Golf Club (8 min), Marina Del Rey (17 min), Cabo San Lucas Country Club (21 min), Cabo del Sol Golf (28 min), Santa Maria Beach (34 min), Chileno Beach (37 min)
  • Land's End and The Arch, plus Lover's Beach, are noted nearby landmarks
