Athens · Piraeus

Day in port to be confirmed

Half-Day Private Athens City Tour — Skip the Line at the Acropolis

  • Private · your group only
  • 4 hours · starts 7:30 a.m.–12:30 p.m.
  • Pickup at the port
  • Acropolis skip-the-line
  • Cruise-friendly refund

Athens in half a day, built around the one thing that can't be skipped. Your driver collects you at the port and the Acropolis comes first, early, with skip-the-line entry so you're on the rock while the coach groups are still queuing below. Then the drive-by circuit of the center with stops where you want them: the Temple of Zeus and Hadrian's Arch, the Panathenaic Stadium where the first modern Olympics were run on white marble, the changing of the Evzones at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier, the Academy on Panepistimiou. It finishes in the Plaka and Monastiraki, the old quarter below the Acropolis, with time for a coffee or a wander before you're back at the ship. Tailor-made: your driver adjusts the route to what you want to see.

Not included: Acropolis entry, paid on site. Comfortable shoes for the climb; the path is marble and worn smooth.

Cancellation: full refund up to 1 day before.

Private Sunset at Cape Sounion & the Athens Riviera

  • Private · your group only
  • 5½ hours
  • Pickup at the port
  • Easy walking

The road south from Piraeus runs along the Saronic coast past Glyfada, Vouliagmeni, and Varkiza before it ends at the cliff where the Temple of Poseidon has stood since 440 BC. You arrive with light to spare, walk the marble columns, then watch the sun go down behind them. On the way back your driver stops at a waterfront taverna for grilled fish and a glass of something cold. Private first-class vehicle for the two of you, Wi‑Fi and bottled water on board, English-speaking driver who knows the history.

Timing: October pickup is around 5:00 p.m., so the evening runs to roughly 10:30 p.m. A morning version (quieter temple, seaside lunch instead of dinner) is available if the ship sails that evening.

Not included: €20 per person temple entry, paid on site · dinner and drinks · gratuities. Shoulders covered at the temple — nothing sleeveless.

Cancellation: full refund up to 1 day before.

Santorini · Fira

Sunday, November 1, 2026

Private Highlights of Santorini — Skip the Line

  • Private · your group only
  • 5 hours
  • Limited-mobility friendly
  • Cruise-friendly refund

Santorini on a ship day is a timing problem: tender lines, a cable car queue, and thousands of other passengers heading the same direction at the same hour. This tour solves it with your own vehicle and skip-the-line access, so the day is spent looking rather than waiting. You start at Akrotiri, the Bronze Age town buried under ash 3,600 years ago and excavated with its frescoes and two-story houses intact. Then up to Profitis Ilias, the island's high point, for the whole caldera in one view; the Three Bells of Fira for the photograph everyone wants; and a glass of Assyrtiko at a winery before the drop back in Fira. Morning starts from 8:00 to 11:00 a.m., set to the tender schedule.

Good for: the whole group, including anyone who'd rather not do stairs. Wheelchair-friendly with limited mobility.

Cancellation: full refund up to 1 day before.

Chania · Crete

Monday, November 2, 2026

Cruise-Friendly Chania Tour

  • Group tour
  • 5 hours
  • Built around ship hours
  • Cruise-friendly refund

Ships dock at Souda Bay, 20 minutes from the town, which is the first thing this tour handles for you. The day runs in three parts. An ancient site first, then Agia Triada, a working 17th-century monastery in the olive groves of the Akrotiri peninsula where the monks still press their own oil and pour their own wine. Then Chania itself: a Venetian harbor with a lighthouse at the end of the mole, a domed Ottoman mosque on the quay, and lanes behind it full of leather workshops, knife makers, and the covered Agora market. Guided through all of it, with time on your own at the harbor before the drive back. Starts from 8:30 a.m. to noon, timed to the ship.

Good for: an easy, well-paced town day with a long harbor lunch and no stress about all-aboard.

Cancellation: full refund up to 1 day before.

Katakolon · Olympia

Tuesday, November 3, 2026

Olympia Boutique Expedition: Heritage, Wine & Olive Oil Tasting

  • Small group
  • 5½ hours
  • Pickup at the port
  • Wine & olive oil tastings
  • Cruise-friendly refund

Katakolon exists as a port for one reason: Olympia is 40 minutes inland. The site is a pine-shaded valley where the Games were held for a thousand years, and you walk it at your own pace, past the gymnasium, the Temple of Zeus, and onto the original stadium track where the starting line is still set in stone. The museum next door holds the Hermes of Praxiteles. Then the day turns toward what the region does now: a stop at a family estate for wine from local grapes and olive oil pressed on the property, with a table of what goes with them. Back to the ship with the afternoon in hand.

Walking: the site is flat but exposed and large; allow a hat and water. Timed to the ship.

Cancellation: full refund up to 2 days before.

Corfu

Wednesday, November 4, 2026

Private Half Day: Angelokastro, Lakones & Palaiokastritsa

  • Private · your group only
  • 5 hours · 9:00 a.m. start
  • Pickup at the port
  • Cruise-friendly refund

The west coast of Corfu is where the island stops being a resort and starts being Greek. The drive climbs through olive groves to Angelokastro, a 13th-century Byzantine fortress on a sea cliff roughly 300 meters up, with views back along the coast that explain why it was never taken. Lakones, the village below, has the terrace everyone photographs Palaiokastritsa from. Then you drop to the water itself: the monastery on the headland, six small coves, and if you want it, a short boat ride into the sea caves. Back at the ship with time to spare.

Walking: Angelokastro is a steep 10–15 minute climb on uneven steps. Good shoes; the view is the reward.

Cancellation: full refund up to 3 days before.

Split

Thursday, November 5, 2026

Private Split & Trogir: Wine Tasting & Vineyard with Sea View

  • Private · your group only
  • 5 hours
  • Pickup at the port
  • Cruise-friendly refund

Split's old town is a Roman emperor's retirement palace that the city grew into: Diocletian's cellars, the peristyle where the cathedral was built from his mausoleum, and apartments and cafés tucked into walls that are 1,700 years old. Your guide walks you through it before the tour buses arrive. Then it's 30 minutes up the coast to Trogir, a small island town of Venetian stone with one of the best cathedral portals on the Adriatic. The last stop is the one the ladies will talk about: a hillside vineyard at Kaštela with the sea below the terrace, a flight of Dalmatian wines, and a plate of local food to go with them. Back to the ship with time to spare.

Good for: history in the morning, a long glass of wine with a view in the afternoon, and no one else's schedule to follow. Start times from 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., set to the ship.

Cancellation: full refund up to 1 day before.

Dubrovnik

Friday, November 6, 2026

Private Food & Wine Day: Dubrovnik, Cavtat & Konavle

  • Private · your group only
  • 6 hours · 9:00 a.m. start
  • Pickup at the port
  • Food & wine tastings
  • Cruise-friendly refund

Most ship passengers never leave the old city walls. This day goes the other direction. It opens on Srđ Hill, the ridge above Dubrovnik where the whole walled city sits below you like a model, then follows the Ombla, a short river that rises from a spring and runs straight into the sea. South along the coast is Cavtat, a small harbor town with a palm-lined waterfront and none of Dubrovnik's crowds, walked on foot with your guide. Then inland into the Konavle valley, where family farms still cure their own pršut, press their own olive oil, and pour wine from vines a few steps from the table. Expect a long, unhurried lunch and the kind of hospitality that doesn't appear on a menu.

Good for: the two of you sitting at a table with locals rather than standing in line for the city walls. Come hungry.

Cancellation: full refund up to 7 days before — the firmest policy on this page, so this one is worth deciding early.

Kotor · Montenegro

Saturday, November 7, 2026

Best of Montenegro Private Day Tour from Kotor Port

  • Private · your group only
  • 6½ hours
  • Starts 7:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.
  • Cruise-friendly refund

The ship sails into the Bay of Kotor at dawn, which is the best view of the day and already paid for. This tour gives you the rest of the country. The road climbs the serpentine above the bay, 25 hairpins with the fjord shrinking below you at every turn, to Cetinje, the old royal capital, and its monastery, where Montenegro's kings were crowned and relics are still kept. Then down to the Adriatic side: the Budva Riviera, a walled old town on a small peninsula, and the stop at Sveti Stefan, the fortified fishing island turned resort that is the photograph on every Montenegro postcard. Back in Kotor with time to wander the medieval walls before all-aboard.

Good for: three very different sides of a small country in one day, with the driving and the timing handled for you.

Cancellation: full refund up to 2 days before.

Rome Catacombs & Appian Way: Private Golf Cart Tour

  • Private · your group only
  • 4 hours · 2:00 p.m. start

  • Cruise-friendly refund

Everyone does the Colosseum. Almost no one does what's behind it. This one picks you up in central Rome in an electric golf cart, rolls past the Colosseum, the Forum, the Arch of Constantine, and Circus Maximus for the photographs, then leaves the traffic behind on the Via Appia Antica, the original Roman road, still paved with its basalt blocks and lined with umbrella pines, tombs, and the Aurelian Walls. The stop is the Catacombs, where you go underground with a guide into the tunnels where early Christians buried their dead. An aqueduct on the way back. Four hours, nothing crowded, nothing on foot that you don't choose.

Logistics: pickup is in central Rome, not at the pier. The 90-minute transfer from Civitavecchia and back is arranged separately — Christina will quote it alongside whichever Rome option you choose.

Rome Catacombs & Capuchin Crypts Tour with Transport

  • Group tour · max 25
  • 3½ hours · 10:10 a.m. start
  • Private van between sites
  • Cruise-friendly refund

Rome from below, in the morning. The Capuchin Crypt under Via Veneto is six small chapels decorated entirely with the bones of some 3,700 friars, arranged into arches, rosettes, and a clock, with a guide to explain why the Capuchins saw it as a meditation rather than a horror. From there a van takes you out past the Aurelian Walls to the Catacomb of Callixtus on the Appian Way, the largest of Rome's early Christian cemeteries, with 12 miles of tunnels and the crypt where nine 3rd-century popes were buried. The only Rome option on this page that starts in the morning, which leaves the afternoon free.

Logistics: meets in Rome, not at the pier. The 90-minute transfer from Civitavecchia and back is arranged separately — Christina will quote it alongside whichever Rome option you choose. A 10:10 a.m. start means leaving the ship early.

The private version of underground Rome, and the one that goes deepest. Three sites, your own guide, nobody else. San Clemente is a 12th-century basilica built on a 4th-century church built on a 1st-century Roman house with a Mithraic temple in its basement; you walk down through all of it, level by level, with running water audible at the bottom. The Catacombs of Domitilla are the oldest and least visited of Rome's catacombs, with a sunken 4th-century basilica inside them. The Capuchin Crypt finishes the morning: six chapels under Via Veneto decorated with the bones of 3,700 friars. Three hours, no queue, no group to wait for.
