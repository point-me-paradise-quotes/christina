The Trip

Drive to the pier. Sail on the largest ship afloat.

No airport, no connections, no lost luggage — Galveston is a two-hour drive from most of Houston.

Icon of the Seas moves to Galveston in August 2027, and this October sailing is one of its early Texas departures. It carries the largest waterpark at sea, a suspended infinity pool, an ice rink, and eight distinct neighborhoods — Surfside for families, Chill Island for adults, Central Park in the middle of it all. It is less a ship than a small coastal town that happens to relocate overnight.

The route is built around three very different days ashore. Cozumel for reef diving and a short ferry to the mainland. Costa Maya for the quieter Yucatán coast, where the beach clubs at Mahahual are a ten-minute cab from the pier. Perfect Day at CocoCay in the Bahamas, Royal Caribbean's private island, where a full day costs you nothing extra unless you want the waterpark or a cabana.

Four sea days are woven through the eight nights — one early, one mid-route, and two on the long westward run home from the Bahamas. On a ship this size, those are the days the ship itself becomes the destination.

Day by Day

The Route

All times local. Ports of call may vary by departure date and are subject to weather and other conditions.

Day 1
Oct 9
Galveston, Texas
Depart 4:30 p.m.

Terminal doors open early afternoon. Aim to be checked in by 2:00 p.m. so you have time to find your muster station, drop your carry-on, and be on deck for the sail-away past the jetties.

Day 2
Oct 10
At Sea

First full day. This is when you book the shows — Aqua Dome and the ice theater fill fast on the opening sea day.

Day 3
Oct 11
Cozumel, Mexico
8:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m.

Ten hours is a generous port day. Long enough for a two-tank dive on Palancar, a beach club on the west shore, or the ferry across to Playa del Carmen and back.

Day 4
Oct 12
Puerto Costa Maya, Mexico
8:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.

The port sits about 350 miles south of Cancún, near the Belize border. Mahahual village and its beach clubs are a short ride from the pier; Chacchoben's Mayan ruins are about an hour inland.

Day 5
Oct 13
At Sea

The crossing east toward the Bahamas. A good day for the Solarium, a specialty lunch, or the thermal suite.

Day 6
Oct 14
Perfect Day at CocoCay, Bahamas
8:30 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.

Royal Caribbean's private island. Beaches, the freshwater pool, and lunch are included with your fare. Thrill Waterpark, Coco Beach Club, and cabanas are add-ons — and they sell out months ahead.

Day 7
Oct 15
At Sea

Westbound. The long return through the Florida Straits and into the Gulf begins.

Day 8
Oct 16
At Sea

Last full day. Settle the onboard account, pack the night bag, and take one more run at whatever you missed.

Day 9
Oct 17
Galveston, Texas
Arrive 6:00 a.m.

Early arrival. Self-carry walk-off usually begins around 7:00 a.m. — most guests are in their cars and headed north by mid-morning.
