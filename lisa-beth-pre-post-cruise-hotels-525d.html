Electra Metropolis, Athens

Mitropoleos Street, two blocks off Syntagma Square
Athens · Historic Center

Electra Metropolis

Check in Thursday, Oct 29 · check out Saturday, Oct 31
Rooftop pool & bar Breakfast buffet included Free Wi-Fi 5 min walk to Plaka Syntagma metro, 3 min Airport transfer included

You land at 4:35 p.m., which is exactly late enough that the smart move is to do nothing ambitious. Drop your bags, take the lift to the rooftop, and let the first thing you see in Greece be the Parthenon lit up above the rooftops with a drink in your hand.

The hotel sits on Mitropoleos, the street running from Syntagma Square down to the cathedral — the seam between modern Athens and the old town. Turn left out the door and you're at the Parliament building for the changing of the guard. Turn right and you're in Plaka in five minutes, on the marble lanes below the Acropolis where the tavernas put tables out under the bougainvillea. Breakfast is included in every room type below and worth getting up for.

Hotel Barocco, Rome

Piazza Barberini, at the top of the Trevi neighborhood
Rome · Piazza Barberini

Hotel Barocco

Check in Tuesday, Nov 10 · check out Friday, Nov 13
8 min walk to the Trevi Fountain Barberini metro, Line A, 1 min 10 min to the Spanish Steps Small — 41 rooms Breakfast included Port transfer included

You disembark in the morning, transfer about ninety minutes into the city, and arrive with the whole evening free. That's deliberate. Rome after dark on your first night — the Trevi Fountain floodlit and half as crowded, the Pantheon's portico open to the sky — beats anything you could have scheduled.

The Barocco is a small hotel on the corner of Piazza Barberini, with Bernini's Triton Fountain in the square outside and Via Veneto climbing away behind it. Forty-one rooms, a staff that knows you by the second morning, breakfast in a vaulted room downstairs. What you're buying is position: the metro is a minute away for the Vatican and the Colosseum, and everything in the centro storico is a walk.

Est. $92.79 separates these for the whole stay. The Classic is a double bed only; the Superior comes in either configuration. Both include breakfast and Wi-Fi, both are held with a card guarantee and charge automatically on November 1, and both are fully refundable through November 8.
