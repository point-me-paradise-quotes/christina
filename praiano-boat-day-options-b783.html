Two directions from Praiano

Your hotel can arrange a private boat for you, with a skipper, for either a half day or a full day at sea. Everything below is exclusive to your party. No other guests, no fixed route, no schedule but your own.

The first decision is not which boat. It is which way you point.

Choosing your day

Two genuinely different experiences. One is a crossing to an island. The other is a slow reading of the coastline you are already staying on.

Capri  /  full day only

The island crossing

Best if Capri is on the list and you have not yet seen it

You leave the coast behind, round Punta Campanella and cross open water to the island. The boat circles Capri, passing beneath the Faraglioni sea stacks, then anchors somewhere quiet to swim before you go ashore.

  • Roughly one hour each way on a traditional boat, closer to forty minutes on a fast one
  • Time ashore in Capri town or Anacapri, on foot
  • Swim stops in the coves along the island's south side
  • Offered as a full day only. The crossing does not fit a half day
Amalfi Coast  /  half or full day

The coastline

Best for swimming, shorter runs and a slower pace

You follow the cliffs in both directions from Praiano. Positano rising out of its ravine to the west, the Fiordo di Furore and Amalfi to the east, with the whole day punctuated by swim stops in water you cannot reach from the road.

  • Positano, the Li Galli islets, Fiordo di Furore, Conca dei Marini, Amalfi and Atrani
  • Short runs between stops, so more time in the water and less time under way
  • The better choice with children, or if anyone is uneasy in open sea
  • Optional stop ashore in Positano or Amalfi

Choosing your boat

Six boats across three quite different characters. The price gap between them is mostly about deck space and shade, not about the quality of the day.

The traditional gozzo

Aprea, built in Sorrento

The wooden hulled fishing boat of the Bay of Naples, adapted for pleasure. Broad in the beam, canopied against the sun, and stable in a swell. It moves at an unhurried pace, which is rather the point. This is the boat most people are picturing when they imagine this coast. Our usual recommendation.

The sport yacht

Pershing 37

Low, sleek and considerably faster, from the Italian yard that more or less invented the look. It cuts the Capri crossing to around forty minutes, which buys you well over an hour more on the island. The trade is real: an open cockpit with little shade, and a firmer ride once the afternoon wind gets up.

The small open boat

Lancia C100

A simple open launch with a skipper. No canopy, no frills, and closer to how people here actually get up and down this coast. It is the least expensive way onto the water and perfectly pleasant for two, though it is a coastal boat rather than a crossing boat.

Worth knowing before you choose

Departure times

Half day morning runs 9:00 to 1:00 or 10:00 to 2:00. Half day afternoon runs 2:00 to 6:00. Full days run 10:00 to 6:00.

Priority docking on Capri

The €200 option secures a faster berth at Marina Grande. On a busy summer afternoon that can save the better part of an hour. If you do not plan to go ashore, it is unnecessary.

The Blue Grotto

It closes without warning whenever there is any swell, and entry is by small rowing boat with a separate ticket. Treat it as a happy possibility rather than a fixture of the day.

Lunch

Several of the best places on Capri and along the coast can only be reached by sea, and they book out well ahead in season. Tell us if you would like lunch built into the day and we will arrange it.

Weather

Your skipper makes the final call on sea conditions. If a day is called off for weather it is rescheduled where possible. We will confirm the exact terms in writing.

Cancellation

Free cancellation up to two days before your tour.

How to hold your day

Tell us two things: which boat, and which date during your stay. We will confirm availability with the hotel and hold it for you.

These boats go quickly. There are only six of them and one of each, so the popular dates commit weeks ahead in high season. If a boat day matters to you, it is worth deciding early rather than waiting until you arrive.
