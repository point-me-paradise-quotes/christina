The short version

Same ship, same Yacht Club, nine Saturdays to choose from — the only real decisions are the date, the route, and how much suite you want.

Every rate on this page is for one MSC Yacht Club suite with two guests total. MSC World America runs two seven-night loops out of PortMiami all winter: a Western Caribbean week through Roatán, Costa Maya, and Cozumel, and an Eastern Caribbean week through Puerto Plata and San Juan. Both finish with a full day at Ocean Cay, MSC's private island in the Bahamas, before docking back in Miami at 7:00 AM on Saturday.

Pick a row below and the running total at the bottom of the page updates. The suite tiers quoted vary by sailing because that's what MSC had available when these rates were pulled — if you want a tier that isn't shown on your preferred date, say so in the notes and I'll check it.

What the Yacht Club actually is

The Yacht Club is a keycard-access section of the ship — 152 suites on the forward upper decks with their own restaurant, lounge, pool deck, and concierge desk. You still have the run of the whole ship (Eataly, Butcher's Cut, the World Promenade, the theatre); the Yacht Club is where you go when you don't want it. Every suite tier on this page, including the Interior Suite, comes with the full package below. The honest trade across tiers is space and a balcony, not service — the butler and the lounge are the same whether you're in 160 square feet or 600.

  • 24-hour butler and a dedicated concierge team
  • Premium Extra drink package, usable throughout the ship
  • Premium internet package included
  • Private Yacht Club Restaurant for breakfast, lunch, and dinner
  • Top Sail Lounge and a private pool deck with whirlpools
  • Thermal suite access at the MSC Aurea Spa
  • Priority embarkation and disembarkation
  • Ocean House beach, bars, and restaurant at Ocean Cay — Yacht Club guests only

Which week?

The honest trade between the two routes, so you're choosing on purpose.

Western: more ports, less lounge

Roatán, Costa Maya, and Cozumel on three consecutive days means three mornings with somewhere to be — reef snorkeling, Mayan ruins, a beach club — and only two sea days to use the Yacht Club pool deck you're paying for. Ocean Cay runs 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

Two dates quoted: January 16 and February 13. Both depart Miami at 5:00 PM.

Eastern: fewer ports, longer days

Puerto Plata and San Juan back to back, then two full sea days before Ocean Cay. San Juan's 11:00 PM departure is the standout — enough time for dinner in Old San Juan without watching the clock. The 4:30 PM Miami departure is 30 minutes earlier than the Western week.

Seven dates quoted, January 23 through April 17. The two April sailings run a shorter Ocean Cay day (7:00 AM to 6:00 PM); the Interior Suite rates on those dates are the lowest on the page.

The suites

Four tiers appear across these nine sailings. Here's what each one buys you — sizes are MSC's published figures.

Category YIN

MSC Yacht Club Interior Suite

The entry point into the Yacht Club, and the one most people underestimate. No window, but every privilege — the butler, the lounge, the restaurant, the drink and internet packages, Ocean House. If you plan to live on the Top Sail deck rather than in the room, this is the smart money. Only seven of these on the ship.

  • Approx. 160 sq ft (15 sqm)
  • Double bed, convertible to twins · max 2 guests
  • Forward, decks 15–16
  • Shower bathroom, wardrobe, mini bar
Category YC1

MSC Yacht Club Deluxe Suite

The standard Yacht Club suite and the one with the most inventory — 130 of them across the forward decks. Roughly 70 percent more floor space than the Interior Suite, plus a private balcony. On most dates the step up from Interior runs $1,000 to $1,600 for the week.

  • Approx. 270 sq ft (25 sqm) + 50 sq ft balcony
  • Double bed, convertible to twins
  • Forward, decks 15–21
  • Large shower, spacious wardrobe, mini bar
Category YC3 · quoted January 16 only

MSC Yacht Club Royal Suite with Whirlpool Bath

A proper apartment at sea: the balcony alone is bigger than the entire Deluxe Suite, with its own whirlpool bath and an outdoor dining table. Walk-in wardrobe, a bathroom with both tub and large shower, and an espresso machine in the room.

  • Approx. 549–624 sq ft (51–58 sqm) + 452–549 sq ft balcony
  • Double bed · up to 4 guests
  • Forward, decks 16 and 18
  • Private whirlpool bath on the balcony
Category YJD · quoted February 13 only

MSC Yacht Club Duplex Suite with Whirlpool Bath

Two levels — open living and dining room below, master bedroom with a tub bathroom above — and a large private balcony with a whirlpool and sundeck. Only six exist on the ship. Less interior floor than the Royal Suite but a two-story layout that feels like a townhouse.

  • Approx. 495 sq ft (46 sqm) over two levels + 300–335 sq ft balcony
  • Master bedroom upstairs, double sofa bed downstairs
  • Forward, deck 16
  • Private whirlpool bath and dining table on the balcony

The ports

A sentence or two on each stop — the Trip Guide will go deeper once you've picked a date.

Western · Day 3

Isla de Roatán

The largest of Honduras' Bay Islands, sitting on the Mesoamerican Reef. Nine hours in port, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM — enough for West Bay Beach and a snorkel straight off the sand.

Western · Day 4

Costa Maya

A purpose-built cruise port on the southern Quintana Roo coast near the village of Mahahual. The draw is Chacchoben, a Mayan site about an hour inland, or a beach club on the Malecón. In port 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Western · Day 5

Cozumel

Mexico's best-known dive island, off the Yucatán coast opposite Playa del Carmen. Palancar and Columbia reefs on the west side; the long 7:00 PM departure leaves room for a late beach afternoon.

Eastern · Day 3

Puerto Plata

The Dominican Republic's north coast, with the cable car up Mount Isabel de Torres and the Victorian-era center a short ride from the pier. 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Eastern · Day 4

San Juan

The ship docks on the edge of Old San Juan, so El Morro, the blue-cobbled streets, and dinner are all on foot. The 11:00 PM departure is the best port day on either route for that reason.

Both · Day 7

Ocean Cay MSC Marine Reserve

MSC's private island in the Bahamas, about 65 miles east of Miami. As a Yacht Club guest you have Ocean House — its own beach, bars, cabanas, and restaurant — separate from the main island.
