Courtyard by Marriott Oahu North ShoreNorth Shore, Lāʻie · 1 room · $3,627.87 · free cancellation until Nov 21

Courtyard by Marriott Oahu North Shore

Lāʻie, windward North Shore · 7 nights, December 21–28 · about 67 minutes' drive from Honolulu airport

The quiet choice. A low-rise, three-floor Courtyard beside the Polynesian Cultural Center, fully renovated in late 2024, with Laniloa Beach a two-minute walk across Kamehameha Highway. Waimea Bay is 13 miles up the road, the Banzai Pipeline 11. It is a well-run, clean base for a North Shore week — not a resort. One restaurant, no spa, no room service, no bell staff, no shuttle.

Two things to know before you choose it: the on-site Bistro serves no alcohol (Lāʻie is a dry-adjacent community), and a rental car is essential — there is no shuttle and no practical transit.

What's on site

  • Outdoor pool, children's pool and hot tub (7:00 AM–10:00 PM)
  • Direct beach access; beach and water-sports gear rented at the activity desk
  • 24-hour fitness center, pickleball, basketball, volleyball, firepit
  • The Bistro (American, alcohol-free) and a Starbucks-serving café
  • Self-service guest laundry, convenience store, daily housekeeping
  • Wi-Fi is complimentary in the quoted rooms

Hilton Hawaiian Village Waikiki Beach Resort

Waikiki Beach · 7 nights, December 21–28 · about 16 minutes' drive from Honolulu airport

The big Waikiki resort, and the one where you can leave the car behind. Twenty-two oceanfront acres on the widest stretch of beach in Waikiki, five outdoor pools, a waterslide, a hot tub, a poolside bar and an American restaurant, and Mandara Spa with a steam room and sauna. Ko Olina Beach VillasKo Olina, leeward coast · two-bedroom villa · $8,004.00

Ko Olina Beach Villas

Ko Olina, leeward coast · 7 nights, December 21–28 · two-bedroom, two-bath ocean-view villa in a gated resort community

A home rather than a room, set high above the lagoons on the leeward coast. Wide sliding doors open the living room to a private lanai with the Pacific in front of you, and the kitchen — stainless appliances, stone counters, a full set of cookware, a wine fridge — makes eating in easy. A dining table seats six, a breakfast bar handles the in-between meals, and there's a washer and dryer for a week's worth of beach towels. Outside the door: lagoon-style pools, hot tubs, a fitness center, and paths to Ko Olina's four calm swimming lagoons, the resort restaurants, and Aulani.

The villa and the resort

  • Primary suite: king bed, flatscreen TV, ensuite with walk-in shower and deep soaking tub
  • Second bedroom: two twin beds (convertible to a king on request), adjacent full bath with tub/shower
  • Open living room with large flatscreen, dining for six, breakfast bar
  • Central air conditioning, high-speed Wi-Fi, in-villa washer and dryer
  • Lagoon pools, hot tubs, fitness center, oceanfront paths; golf and marina a short drive
