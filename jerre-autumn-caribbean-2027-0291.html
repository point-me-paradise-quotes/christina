The sailing

Drive to the port. No flights, no transfers, no airport mornings.

Everything on this page is priced for one stateroom, two adults — Jerre and Autumn — sailing round-trip from Galveston on Sunday, May 30, 2027 and returning Sunday, June 6. Pick the stateroom that fits, decide whether you want drinks and Wi-Fi bundled in, and the total updates as you go.

About the ship. MSC Seascape entered service in late 2022 and has homeported in Galveston since November 2025, sailing this same Western Caribbean loop every Sunday. It is one of MSC's larger ships, built for warm-weather itineraries — a long stretch of open-air deck space, a promenade that wraps the outside of the ship rather than running through the middle of it, and a wide choice of included and specialty dining.

What do Bella, Fantastica, and Aurea mean? Every stateroom below carries one of these labels — tap to see what each one includes.
Level 1 · lowest fare

Bella

You pick the category. MSC picks the cabin.
  • Guaranteed stateroom in your chosen category — Interior, Ocean View, or Balcony
  • Deck and location assigned by MSC, usually a few weeks before sailing
  • Full access to included dining, shows, pools, and deck activities
The honest trade: the best price, in exchange for not knowing exactly where on the ship you'll be.
Level 2 · most categories

Fantastica

You choose the deck and the cabin.
  • Choose your deck, location, and stateroom number at booking
  • Available on Interior, Ocean View, and Balcony staterooms
  • Everything in Bella
Typically $100–$200 per stateroom above Bella on this sailing. Worth it if you care about being midship, high up, or away from the elevators.
Level 3 · balconies & suites

Aurea

MSC's premium experience on this ship.
  • Choose your stateroom, as with Fantastica
  • Flexible dining — eat when you like rather than at a fixed seating
  • Priority check-in and boarding in Galveston
  • Access to the spa's thermal area throughout the cruise
  • Bathrobe and slippers in the stateroom
Roughly $400–$560 per stateroom above the same balcony as Fantastica. All four suite categories are Aurea by default.

What the fare covers

Both fares cover the ship. One of them also covers the bar tab and the Wi-Fi.

Cruise Only

  • Main dining room and buffet
  • Entertainment and production shows
  • Pools and deck activities
  • Kids' clubs

Drinks & Wi-Fi Included

  • Everything in Cruise Only
  • Drink package for both guests
  • Wi-Fi package

Adds $628 per stateroom — about $45 per person, per day. If you'd normally have a couple of drinks by the pool and one with dinner, it usually comes out ahead; if you rarely drink, Cruise Only is the better buy.

Not included in either fare: specialty dining, shore excursions, gratuities, travel insurance, and transfers. I'll walk you through each of these once you've picked a stateroom — some are worth pre-booking, some aren't.
