The case against Basic on this trip

Basic saves $44 per person each way against Choice — $176 round trip for the two of you on most flights below. Here's what that $176 buys back:

  • A seat you chose. Basic assigns your seat at check-in, from whatever's left. With two of you on the same booking, there is no guarantee you sit together.
  • The right to change. A Basic ticket can't be moved to a different flight or day. If Orlando plans shift by an hour, the only fix is buying up to a higher fare at that day's price.
  • A credit that's actually usable. Cancel a Basic fare and you get a credit that dies in six months and can't be handed to anyone else. Cancel Choice and it lives twelve months and can be transferred.
  • Same-day flexibility. Finish early, want the 1:55 p.m. instead of the 7:45 p.m.? Choice lets you switch same-day to an open seat between the same cities. Basic doesn't.

Our read: Choice is the floor for this trip. Step up to Choice Preferred if you want the ticket fully refundable and a seat toward the front. Step up to Choice Extra if you're checking two bags — the included bags and the legroom close most of the price gap on their own.
