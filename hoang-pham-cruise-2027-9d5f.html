The trip at a glance

A graduation deserves a real send-off, and this one starts an easy drive from home. Symphony of the Seas leaves Galveston on Sunday afternoon and gives you six nights on the Western Caribbean: a full sea day to settle into the ship, three port days in Mexico and Honduras, and a second sea day to finish before you're back in Texas on Saturday morning.

Symphony is one of the largest ships at sea, with seven distinct neighborhoods, a boardwalk with a carousel, a central park lined with real trees, and a 10-story dry slide called the Ultimate Abyss. For three graduating seniors, that's a lot of ship to work through in six nights. The two stateroom options below are priced for all three of you together.

Day by day

Port times are the ship's schedule as quoted today. Plan to be back aboard at least 30 minutes before the posted departure.

  • Day 1
    Galveston, Texas
    Sunday, May 30, 2027 · Sail away 4:00 p.m.
    Boarding runs through the early afternoon. Galveston's terminal is about an hour from central Houston without traffic; allow more on a Sunday in late May.
  • Day 2
    At sea
    Monday, May 31, 2027
    The day to learn the ship: the FlowRider surf simulator, the zip line over the Boardwalk, the Ultimate Abyss slide, and the Central Park cafés that are quieter than the pool deck at noon.
  • Day 3
    Puerto Costa Maya, Mexico
    Tuesday, June 1, 2027 · 11:59 a.m. – 7:00 p.m.
    A late arrival, so mornings are yours. The port village is purpose-built; the reasons to leave it are the Mayan ruins at Chacchoben (about an hour inland) and the small fishing town of Mahahual, a ten-minute taxi down the coast, where the beach clubs sit on the sand under palapas.
  • Day 4
    Roatán, Honduras
    Wednesday, June 2, 2027 · 7:00 a.m. – 3:00 p.m.
    An early call and an early departure — eight hours. Roatán sits on the Mesoamerican Reef, and West Bay Beach has the clearest snorkeling of the three ports straight off the sand. It's about 35 minutes from the pier.
  • Day 5
    Cozumel, Mexico
    Thursday, June 3, 2027 · 7:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.
    Ten hours in the busiest cruise port in Mexico. Palancar and Columbia reefs on the southwest side are the draw for snorkelers and divers; the beach clubs along the same coast cater to ship days.
  • Day 6
    At sea
    Friday, June 4, 2027
    A last full day aboard. Pack in the evening — bags go outside your door the night before arrival.
  • Day 7
    Galveston, Texas
    Saturday, June 5, 2027 · Morning arrival
    Your advisor will confirm the arrival time once the line publishes it.
