The shape of the trip

One night in Papeete to land, then six on Moorea with nothing to pack up in between.

Brandi, here is the honeymoon as it stands: United from Houston to Tahiti, a first night in Papeete steps from the ferry, and six nights over the lagoon on Moorea at the Sofitel Kia Ora. Everything below is priced for one room and two adults.

Before you choose

Six nights or eight. This quote is built on six nights on Moorea. Eight nights is also an option if you would like more time on the island. Flight schedules from Houston line up best with a six- or eight-night Moorea stay, so those are the two lengths worth pricing.

Flying via Los Angeles instead. It can be done, with trade-offs: longer layovers and an early-morning arrival in Tahiti. Hotel check-in is not until the afternoon, and the ferry adds more travel to an already long journey, so it makes for a harder first day. The upside is that a morning arrival lets you skip the Papeete overnight and go straight to Moorea if you'd prefer.

A rental car on Moorea. Highly recommended. I add this separately, and it is paid on the island, so it is not part of the package rates below.

Your first night in Papeete

You land after dark. Sleep in town, cross to Moorea in daylight.

Included in every package · 1 night · Sat, Mar 13 · 1 room · 2 adults

Boutique Hotel Kon Tiki Tahiti

Central Papeete, a short walk from the Moorea ferry.

After fourteen hours of travel, the job of this night is simple: a short ride from the airport, a king bed, and the ferry terminal a few minutes away on foot the next morning. Kon Tiki sits on Boulevard de la Reine Pomare IV in the middle of Papeete, about ten minutes by car from the airport.

There are two places to eat upstairs — SURFHOUSE, and the REEFTOP rooftop bar, both looking out over the water. Each is open select days and takes reservations, so I'll check what is serving on a Saturday night before you go.

A minibus van meets you at the airport on arrival evening and takes you to the hotel; that transfer is in every package. The ferry terminal is walkable the next morning. If you'd rather not walk with luggage, I can add a short transfer to the ferry.

Your resort · Northeast coast at Temae · 6 nights

Sofitel Kia Ora Moorea Beach Resort

Temae's white sand on the northeast coast, facing Tahiti across the channel.
Resort photos1 / 80

The Sofitel is on the northeast coast at Temae, on the island's longest white-sand beach, with Tahiti's outline across the water. It is about five minutes from the Vaiare ferry dock, and it reopened in 2026 after a renovation of its common areas and overwater villas.

What it does best: crystal-blue water and some of the best snorkeling on the lagoon, straight from your deck, plus the beach and the easiest arrival and departure days on the island.

There are two restaurants. PURE is on the beach and serves breakfast, lunch, and dinner. K is the dinner-only dining room looking out over the ocean; Sofitel's own site currently lists it as unavailable. Vue, the cocktail bar above the pool, serves lunch, dinner, and lighter plates, and there is a second bar down on the sand. The spa has five treatment rooms and outdoor treatment areas, and yoga classes run on site.

The honest trade: Cook's Bay and Opunohu Bay are a drive away, dinner options on site are limited, and the resort loses the light earlier in the day, because the afternoon sun drops behind the mountain that sits right behind it.
