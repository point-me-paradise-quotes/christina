Life inside the Yacht Club

A small, key-card-only enclave at the front of a very large ship.

MSC Seascape · MSC Yacht Club, decks 16–19. Both sailings below are the same ship and the same Yacht Club, so what follows applies to either week. Your suite key opens a private restaurant, the Top Sail Lounge, and a sundeck with its own pool, grill and bar that the rest of the ship can't reach. A butler and a concierge are on call around the clock, and you board and leave the ship through priority lines on both Sundays.

Butler and concierge

24-hour butler service and a dedicated concierge desk, plus priority embarkation and disembarkation.

Drinks included

MSC's Premium Extra Drink package, good in the Yacht Club and at bars and restaurants across the ship. The in-suite minibar is included too.

Wi-Fi included

MSC's Browse internet package with unlimited data, two devices per guest, for the full week.

Private dining

A Yacht Club-only restaurant with flexible dining times, so there's no assigned seating to plan around.

Top Sail Lounge

The Yacht Club's own lounge and bar, with live music and afternoon tea.

Thermal suite

Complimentary access to the thermal area in the MSC Aurea Spa for the length of the cruise.

MSC Yacht Club Deluxe Suite

The simplest way into the Yacht Club.

A balcony suite with a king bed that converts to two singles on request, a seating area, and a bathroom with a large shower. At about 269 sq. ft. (25 sq. m) it's a comfortable room for two who plan to spend their days out in the lounge, on the sundeck and ashore.
