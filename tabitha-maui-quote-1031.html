Where You Stay · 14 Nights

The Westin Maui Resort & Spa, Kaʻanapali

2365 Kaanapali Parkway, Lahaina, HI 96761

Waterfalls and koi ponds run through the entry, and the property opens straight onto Kaʻanapali Beach — two minutes on foot, with loungers, umbrellas, and towels waiting. There are three outdoor pools, including an adults-only pool if you want quiet, and water slides at the other end if you don't. A poolside bar, a beachfront restaurant, a hot tub, and a full-service spa mean you can go a full day without touching the car. Whalers Village, with its shops and restaurants, is the same two-minute walk; the Kaʻanapali Kai golf course is nine minutes on foot.

It's rated 9.2 out of 10 across 1,179 guest reviews, with cleanliness at 9.6 and amenities at 9.4 — consistent with what I hear back from clients I've sent there.

What the room rate includes

  • Your room for all 14 nights
  • Wired in-room internet, included with every room type
  • Beach access with loungers, umbrellas, and towels
  • Three outdoor pools, adults-only pool area, water slides, and hot tub
  • Fitness and health club access, in-room bottled water, internet, phone calls, and resort shuttle service — all bundled into the mandatory resort fee below
One honest note on rate inclusions. Some rate plans at this resort bundle in extras — a daily resort credit, breakfast for two, or evening drinks and hors d'oeuvres.
