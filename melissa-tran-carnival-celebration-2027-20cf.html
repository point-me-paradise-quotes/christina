Melissa — every option below is priced for one stateroom with four guests, sailing on Carnival Celebration out of Miami on Sunday, March 14, 2027.

I compared the nine fares Carnival is currently running on this sailing. The one called Great Rates came in lowest in every cabin category, so that's the fare behind every number here. It carries a reduced deposit — about $490 for the whole cabin, almost identical in every category — and lets you choose your own cabin; the trade is that the deposit is nonrefundable once you book. Taxes and port fees are already in the totals. Gratuities and travel protection are not — those are listed plainly further down.

The stateroom categories run from a standard interior to a balcony, and the differences between them are mostly about floor space and where on the ship you sleep. I've laid out each one with its square footage and the honest trade, so the choice is yours.

About the ship

Carnival Celebration is one of Carnival's Excel-class ships — launched in November 2022, about 183,000 tons and 1,130 feet long, carrying roughly 5,374 guests at double occupancy with about 1,735 crew. The ship is organized into six themed zones (Celebration Central, 820 Biscayne, The Gateway, Summer Landing, The Lido, and The Ultimate Playground), plus a Family Harbor zone built around the family staterooms.

For a family of four, the draws are concentrated on the top decks:

  • BOLT — the roller coaster at sea, about $15 per ride
  • WaterWorks aqua park and a ropes course
  • Five outdoor pools
  • Camp Ocean, the kids' club, located next to the Family Harbor staterooms

Adults get the Center Stage atrium theater, the Punchliner Comedy Club, and Loft 19, an adults-only retreat.

How payment works

  • Deposit: $493.50 for the stateroom on interior and balcony categories, $487.00 on ocean view — roughly $122 per person. Due within 48 hours of booking; nonrefundable and nontransferable under the Great Rates fare.
  • Balance: the remainder of the fare, due December 14, 2026. Because the deposit barely moves between categories, the room you choose shows up almost entirely in this payment.

What the stateroom rate doesn't include

  • Gratuities: $119 per person, so $476 for four. Carnival adds these to your onboard account; they can also be prepaid.
  • Travel protection: Carnival's own Vacation Protection runs $95 each for guests 1–2 and $49 each for guests 3–4 ($288 total). I can also quote an independent policy — see the insurance question in the form below.
  • Onboard extras: BOLT rides, specialty dining, drink packages, and shore excursions are purchased separately.
  • Getting to Miami: no flights or hotel nights are included in these numbers. Say the word and I'll price them.

Note on cabin assignment: prices above were pulled with four guests entered for each category, but Carnival doesn't publish which specific cabin numbers hold four. I'll confirm the berths on the exact cabin before anything is booked.
