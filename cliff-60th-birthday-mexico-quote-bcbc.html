Garza Blanca Preserve Resort & Spa — 4 nights, February 25 – March 1, 2027

Garza Blanca sits on a mountainside inside a nature preserve, its Mediterranean-style buildings stepping down to a private white-sand beach. The infinity pool lines up with the ocean horizon, and drinks come to you at your lounger.

The water in front is clear enough for snorkeling, and you can paddle a kayak out toward Los Arcos. Six restaurants, several right on the sand, put dinner in front of the sunset over the bay. The spa is on the roof.

What you’ll find there

  • Private white-sand beach with free loungers, umbrellas and towels
  • Infinity pool facing the ocean, with drinks brought to your lounger
  • Six restaurants, with beachfront tables for sunset
  • Rooftop spa
  • Snorkeling, kayaking, paddleboarding and beach yoga

Hyatt Ziva Puerto Vallarta — 4 nights, February 25 – March 1, 2027

Hyatt Ziva’s private beach is the part guests talk about most: warm, calm, clear water you can walk straight into, with loungers, umbrellas and towels at no charge and a bar a few steps back from the sand.

Days fill easily — yoga, kayaking, beach volleyball, snorkeling, jet skis if you want speed — and dinner can be taken outside, facing the water. Four room types are quoted here, from an ocean-view king to a one-bedroom suite with its own plunge pool.

What you’ll find there

  • Private beach with calm water; loungers, umbrellas and towels free
  • Beachside bar
  • Yoga, kayaking, volleyball, snorkeling and jet skiing
  • Outdoor dining with ocean views

Hilton Vallarta Riviera All-Inclusive Resort — 4 nights, February 25 – March 1, 2027

If the birthday should include a long afternoon at the spa, this is the one built for it. The Eforea spa has five treatment rooms, including couples’ rooms, plus a steam room and sauna — massages run from deep-tissue to hot stone, with wraps, scrubs and facials. Reviewers single it out more than anything else here.

Beyond the spa: seven restaurants (one of them Asian), a swim-up bar alongside the pool and beach bars, a 24-hour gym with Pilates and yoga classes, and live music or a show most evenings.

What you’ll find there

  • Eforea spa: 5 treatment rooms including couples’ rooms, steam room and sauna
  • 7 restaurants; swim-up, pool and beach bars
  • 24-hour gym, Pilates and yoga classes, beach yoga
  • Volleyball, kayaking, concerts and live shows
  • Free in-room WiFi

Velas Vallarta Suites Resort All Inclusive — 4 nights, February 25 – March 1, 2027

Velas is built around its gardens: three pools and a lazy river wind through tropical planting, with cocktails brought out to your lounger. The spa is small and quiet — three treatment rooms, a steam room and sauna — and massages can be booked on the beach at sunset, with the waves for a soundtrack.

Every room here is a suite, and it is the only resort in this quote with two- and three-bedroom options, which matters if family is coming along. There’s a kids club, and the restaurants face the beach.

What you’ll find there

  • Three pools and a lazy river in tropical gardens, with cocktail service
  • Intimate spa: 3 treatment rooms, steam room, sauna; beach massages
  • Kids club
  • Beachfront restaurants
