For an easy, unhurried stay where the baby sets the pace.

The setting

Velas sits on a wide sweep of sand in Marina Vallarta, the marina's sailboat masts behind the palms and the Sierra Madre green in the distance. It's an all-suite resort of about 345 rooms, and the suites are the point: real kitchens, living areas and terraces, so a family can spread out without feeling stacked. Three pools sit in tropical gardens, one with a waterfall, alongside a lazy river where the drinks come to you.

Why it fits

Of the four, this is the gentlest landing with a baby. The airport is about ten minutes away, the suites are big enough for a crib, naps and adults at once, and in-suite dining runs around the clock. Babysitting can be arranged so the two of you can slip away for dinner.

For the birthday

Walk the marina boardwalk at sunset for a drink or dinner by the water, no car needed. For a bigger night, the Malecón and the restaurants of the Zona Romántica are about 20 minutes south.

The spa

Small and quiet, with three treatment rooms: beachside, deep-tissue and hot-stone massage, facials, scrubs and wraps, then the steam room and sauna. Treatments are not included in this quote.

Worth knowing

There's no adults-only section, so the feel is family-friendly throughout: comfortable and well run rather than design-forward. The kids club takes children from age four.

For a birthday built around big views and a dinner to remember.

The setting

South of town the highway narrows and the jungle comes right down to the water. Garza Blanca climbs a hillside inside its own preserve, with suites looking across Banderas Bay toward Los Arcos. The white-sand beach is private, with loungers, umbrellas and towels set out, and the water suits snorkeling, kayaking and paddleboarding. An infinity pool meets the horizon, six restaurants face the sunset, and the rooftop spa has a full hydrotherapy circuit.

Why it fits

The One Bedroom Suite quoted here is 144 sq m, which gives a family with a baby real room to breathe. The all-inclusive plan leans toward serious food, and babysitting is available with 24 hours' notice. Adults 16 and over have been able to dine at the adults-only restaurants of Hotel Mousai next door on the plan; Christina will confirm that's still current.

For the birthday

Dinner at TierraLuna, reached by a trolley ride up the mountain with the bay below, is the natural centerpiece; Christina will confirm whether it falls under the all-inclusive plan. Old Town, the Malecón and the Zona Romántica are about 15 minutes north for a livelier evening.

Worth knowing

The resort is built on a slope, so expect stairs and elevators rather than flat paths, which matters with a stroller. It's also the farthest of the four from the airport.

For a lively stay with grown-up corners built in.

The setting

Hyatt Ziva wraps around Las Estacas, a private cove sheltered by rock, so the water tends to be calmer than on the open bay. The buildings step down the cliffs of Conchas Chinas and nearly every room faces the water. On the beach: loungers, umbrellas and towels, yoga, kayaking, volleyball and snorkeling, and a beach bar for afterward. Reviewers single out the clear, calm water as the highlight of their stay. Infinity pools sit on several levels, with a spa and around ten restaurants and bars.

Why it fits

The most family-oriented resort of the four, with a full children's program and a kids pool. The adults aren't forgotten: two adults-only pools, one quiet and one with a swim-up bar.

For the birthday

The Zona Romántica, with its cobbled streets, rooftop bars and some of Vallarta's best restaurants, is about 10 minutes away. Dinner in town, back in the room within the hour.

Worth knowing

The mood is upbeat, with nightly shows and DJs, and the swim-up adults pool is within earshot of the family pool. The cliffside layout means some walking between levels.

For splitting the difference between family time and grown-up space.

The setting

The Hilton rises above a small private cove at the foot of the Sierra Madre, 444 rooms, all facing the bay. Two infinity pools sit at the water's edge, with a swim-up bar, a beach bar and a poolside bar, seven restaurants, and a Sky Bar and martini lounge on the upper floors looking straight out over Banderas Bay. Days bring beach yoga, volleyball and kayaking, a 24-hour gym with Pilates and yoga classes; evenings, concerts and live shows. In-room Wi-Fi is free.

Why it fits

The only resort of the four with an entire adults-only tower, with its own pool and swim-up bar. The birthday couple can stay there while the family with the baby stays in the main building: everyone meets for meals and the beach, then retreats to their own space. Christina will confirm which rooms below sit in the adults-only tower.

For the birthday

Sunset cocktails at the Sky Bar, then 10 minutes into the Zona Romántica for dinner. The easiest of the four for moving between resort and town.

The spa

Eforea has five treatment rooms, including couples rooms: deep-tissue, hot-stone and sports massage, manicures and pedicures, wraps and facials, with a steam room and sauna. Reviewers often name it the best part of their stay. Treatments are not included in this quote.

Worth knowing

The beach is pretty but small. Some guests report the adults-pool rule isn't always enforced, and the service and finishes sit a step below the other three.

How to choose

  • For the easiest stay with a baby, big suites and a short airport transfer: Velas Vallarta
  • For jungle views, a standout birthday dinner and a more secluded feel: Garza Blanca Preserve
  • For a lively resort with calm cove water and adult pools: Hyatt Ziva Puerto Vallarta
  • For a true adults-only tower alongside family rooms, close to town: Hilton Vallarta Riviera

About the baby. None of the four kids clubs take babies; all start at age four. Babysitting is what gives the adults time alone, so it's worth booking early once you've chosen.

A bonus for late February. It's peak humpback whale season on Banderas Bay. A morning whale-watching cruise is a memorable way to mark a 60th, and whales are often visible from the resorts' shoreline. Not included
