Your Southwest points The full fare for the two of you is Est. $1,670.86. Your balance covers three of the four one-way segments, leaving roughly one one-way to pay in cash.

You also have a second option. Southwest lets you pay with a combination of points and cash on the same booking — you'll be shown several splits at checkout, starting from as few as 1,000 points. That means you could stretch your balance across all four segments and pay a smaller amount of cash on each, rather than covering three outright and paying full fare on the fourth. Worth knowing: a straight award redemption gets more value per point than a points-and-cash split, so the choice is really flexibility versus efficiency. Tell me which way you'd rather go and I'll price both before we book.



★ And you don't have to pay that in cash either. Southwest isn't an Amex transfer partner and its flights don't show up in the Amex travel portal — but there's a simpler route. Book the ticket directly with Southwest on your Amex card, then apply Membership Rewards points against the charge once it posts, through the Amex app or your online account. Points come out around a cent apiece that way. The advantage over booking through Amex Travel is that the reservation stays with Southwest, which means I can still manage it for you if anything changes.
