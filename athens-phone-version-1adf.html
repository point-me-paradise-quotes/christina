Choosing Your Pace

Every outing is marked with a pace level. Pick the level that suits you and you will only be offered outings at that level. You can choose differently on a different day, and you never have to match anyone else.

TIER 1  ·  Seated & Scenic
Walking
Under half a mile, in short stretches
What it looks like
Private van, boat, scenic drive with photo stops
TIER 2  ·  Steady Stroller
Walking
Half a mile to a mile and a half, in segments
What it looks like
Food and market outings, gardens, museums
TIER 3  ·  Willing Walker
Walking
Two to three miles, uneven ground possible
What it looks like
Longer city tours, ancient sites
What is not in this book. No hiking, no climbing for its own sake, and nothing that needs stairs to take part. Every outing has been checked for restrooms, places to sit, shade, and how you get in and out of the vehicle.

Always an option

  • A quiet day at the hotel. Two full days is plenty of Athens, and nobody has to fill both of them.
  • Going out on your own for a coffee, a wander, or the shops.

What Happens Next

All we need from you

  1. Your pace level. Tier 1, 2 or 3. Choose honestly rather than bravely — the whole point is that nobody spends a day keeping up.
  2. Anything you use to get around. A cane, a stick, a rollator. It changes what we recommend and it is nobody’s business but ours.
  3. Which outings appeal, by letter. A first choice and a second if you have one.
  4. Anything you cannot eat. Several of these days involve a proper meal and the kitchens need warning.
You do not all have to do the same thing. On any given day some of us may be at the Acropolis and others on a boat or in a kitchen. That is entirely fine, and everyone will be looked after either way.

Worth knowing

  • The clocks in Europe go back on 25 October, so it gets dark in Athens in the late afternoon. The outings are built around that.
  • Late October in Athens is usually mild. Bring a light jacket and shoes you have already broken in.
  • Several of these book up two to three months ahead, so the sooner we hear from you the better.
