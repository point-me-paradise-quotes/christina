Your Trip

An anniversary on the caldera

Imerovigli, Santorini · Tuesday, October 13 – Saturday, October 17, 2026

Imerovigli sits at the highest point of the caldera rim, between Fira's noise and Oia's crowds. It is the village people move to once they've been to Santorini a few times. Hotel Sunny Villas is built into the cliff face directly above Skaros Rock — a cluster of restored, whitewashed dwellings stacked down the slope, each one turned toward the volcano and the water.

Mid-October is the island's best-kept trade-off: the days still run warm enough for the pool, the light goes long and gold by late afternoon, and the August crush is gone. Breakfast is cooked to order and can be brought to your terrace — you fill out a card the night before and tell them when.

What's within walking distance

  • Caldera viewpoint — 2 minutes on foot from the property.
  • Imerovigli village center — about 3 minutes; a handful of tavernas and sunset bars, including the one next door.
  • Agios Nikolaos — 10 minutes on foot along the rim path.
  • Fira — roughly 15 minutes' walk south along the caldera, or a short taxi.
  • Fira-to-Oia trail — the trailhead is about 9 minutes away; the full walk to Oia takes 3–4 hours and Imerovigli is the natural starting point.
  • Oia — about 20 minutes by car.
  • Thira airport (JTR) — 11 minutes by car.

Before You Decide

The things worth knowing

Told straight, so nothing surprises you at check-in.
This property is built on a cliff, and there are a lot of stairs. There is no elevator anywhere on the grounds, and the paths between the road, the reception, and the rooms are steep stone steps. Staff carry luggage down for you, and guests consistently say it's worth it — but if either of you has a knee, hip, or balance issue, tell me now and I'll steer us to a rim property with level access instead.

Arrival & departure

  • Check-in 2:00 p.m. to 10:30 p.m.; check-out by 11:30 a.m.
  • The front desk is staffed 8:00 a.m. to 10:30 p.m. only. The property needs your flight details in advance — I handle that with them once you're confirmed.
  • Airport transfer runs €40 per vehicle each way, up to three people, available around the clock. I can book it, or arrange a private car.
  • Thira airport is an 11-minute drive; Athinios ferry port about 10 minutes.

What's included in your rate

  • Cooked-to-order breakfast daily for two — order the night before, eat on your terrace if you'd rather.
  • All taxes and fees shown in the totals above.
  • Pool and poolside bar, open April through October — running for your dates.
  • Shared hot tub, open for October.
  • Luggage service down the stairs to your room.

Paid at the property

  • In-room massage — reservations must be made before arrival, and I'll put the request in for you.
