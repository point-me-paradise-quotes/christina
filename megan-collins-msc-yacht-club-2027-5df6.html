The idea

A week that asks nothing of you

Drive to the port, hand off your bags, and let the Yacht Club handle the rest.

The MSC Yacht Club is a private, keycard-only section at the front of the ship — its own restaurant, lounge, pool deck, and a butler assigned to your suite for the week. You can walk out into the rest of MSC Seascape whenever you like; the rest of the ship cannot walk in. For an anniversary with an 8-year-old along, that balance is the point: the waterpark, kids' club, and full ship are a few decks away when wanted, and a quiet lounge, a private pool, and someone to bring a drink when you both just want to sit.

All suites below are priced for two adults and one child (age 8) sharing one suite on the February 28, 2027 sailing. Drinks and Wi-Fi are included with every option.

The sailing

Western Caribbean, round-trip Galveston

Two sea days out, three ports in a row, one sea day home.

About MSC Seascape. Launched in 2022 and based in Galveston year-round, Seascape is one of MSC's newest ships. The Yacht Club occupies the forward decks 16–19 with its own Top Sail Lounge, dedicated restaurant, grill and bar, and a private sundeck pool up top. Yacht Club suites also carry priority embarkation and disembarkation at the Galveston terminal — a meaningful perk on a Sunday turnaround day.
